07 · PROGRAMMING THE ESP32-S3

Five steps, in order.

1

Wire and power

SDA → GPIO8, SCL → GPIO9. 3.3 V to VCC and RESET on both MCP23017s, common ground. MCP1 A0–A2 to GND (0x20); MCP2 A0 to 3.3 V (0x21).

2

Upload the scanner

Tools → USB CDC On Boot: Enabled. Upload, then open the Serial Monitor at 115200 baud.

3

Verify the scan

Expect devices at 0x20 and 0x21, total 2. Paste the output into the checker above if not.

4

Upload final firmware

USB Mode: USB-OTG (TinyUSB). USB CDC On Boot: Disabled. Upload Mode: UART0 / Hardware CDC. Hold BOOT, tap RESET if the port vanishes.

5

Test both outputs

USB on channel 1, notes 36–67 (GrandOrgue, Electone). 5-pin DIN on channel 3 from GPIO17 through a 220 Ω resistor.

ESP32-S3 and sensor pin assignments · straight order, no swaps

The hall sensors wire to the two MCP23017 chips. The chips reach the ESP32-S3 over two I²C wires.

MCP1 · 0x20
A0, A1, A2 all to GND. Pedals 1–16, notes 36–51.
MCP2 · 0x21
A0 to 3.3 V, A1 and A2 to GND. Pedals 17–32, notes 52–67.

Each hall sensor: output to its MCP pin, the other two wires to 3.3 V and GND. Pins use internal pull-ups and read pressed when a magnet pulls them LOW. Never put 5 V on these pins. Chip pin numbers are for the 28-pin MCP23017 (DIP, SOIC, SSOP); the QFN package is numbered differently.
